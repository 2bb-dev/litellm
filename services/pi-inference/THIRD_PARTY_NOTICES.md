in this service's npm lock and its installed package metadata. Dev-only build tools
are excluded from the final image. Keep upstream license files inside
`node_modules` and this notice with redistributions

Reviewed 98 installed production package entries (97 distinct name/version
pairs): 0BSD: 1, Apache-2.0: 47, BSD-3-Clause: 11, ISC: 3, MIT: 36.
No GPL, AGPL, LGPL, SSPL, BUSL, Enterprise, agent-runtime package, missing license
metadata, or other review-required license was found. The repository license
scanner explicitly accepts 0BSD. This is a dependency review, not legal advice

The Pi tarballs omit their MIT license text; the copies below come from the
immutable npm-published `gitHead` revisions linked with their notice. Three AWS
SDK tarballs omit LICENSE but explicitly declare Apache-2.0; their shared
repository license is reproduced from the locked `@aws-sdk/core` package.
`data-uri-to-buffer` embeds its MIT notice in README. Vendored OpenAI
zod-to-json-schema (ISC), OpenAI qs (BSD-3-Clause), and protobufjs Google schemas
(BSD-3-Clause) have also been inspected and their notices are included

The pinned official Node/Debian base image has its own bundled licenses and
copyright notices; this inventory is the additional npm runtime surface, not
an OS-layer SBOM. No provider pricing or credential-use permission is granted
by these software licenses
